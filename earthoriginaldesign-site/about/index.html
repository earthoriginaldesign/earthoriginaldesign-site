About

Earth Original Design creates long-lasting solutions that are made to endure beyond fashion and short-term movements. Designing against the grain of the industry, Earth Original Design aims to address the experiences of the user and issues relating to the social systems we all use.

Approach

Earth Original Design’s approach to design is to research and develop lateral solutions that connect fields and industries that can offer new, insightful perspectives previously unexplored.

Values

Design beyond the label, and build solutions that stand on their own merit. With the objects lasting long after their release, and the branding acting only as an assurance to the quality users will find in Earth Original Design’s solutions.

Our changes and variations exclusively bring new, fundamental improvements to the design. The current foundational designs of Earth Original Design are deliberately minimal and focused so that they might be produced with the highest level of quality and design strategy.
