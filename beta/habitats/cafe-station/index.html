Habitats / Proposition / P-01

Standing café station

A café experience without a café. Quality coffee early in the morning, late at night, or in a town with no café at all. The space is the design, and the machine is its centrepiece.

Concept Proposition · long-term Spans Habitats + Systems

What becomes possible

Good coffee at hours and places that have none. The setting, sounds and smells of a café, with the standing station as the feature.

Who it is for

The driver stopping in a country town. The student on a study break. A local station that becomes part of the daily ritual, smoother than any café queue or home machine.

The machine

Reliable and industrial, with the presence of a hotel elevator. Some days you will see someone working on it with the side panel off, swapping a part in minutes and refilling what is missing, then making way for people to get on with their day.

Maintenance is part of the experience, not hidden from it.

Open questions

A proposition is honest about what it has not solved yet.

  1. 01Who hosts and owns a station, and who earns from each cup?
  2. 02Food safety and permits for unattended milk and coffee.
  3. 03Restocking, cleaning, weather and wear.
  4. 04A price per cup that feels fair against a café.
