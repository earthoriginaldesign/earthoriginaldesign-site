Studio and charter

Earth Original Design is an independent design practice in Western Australia, founded by industrial designer Oliver Naisbitt.

Purpose

Earth Original Design exists to raise the tide: to design things that expand what people can do with their lives, at every scale from a lamp to a settlement.

Success is not a sleeker version of what already exists. It is something that opens a possibility that was not there before, reached through design.

Commitments

  1. Expand human capability. Give people something meaningful they could not previously do, access or sustain.
  2. Make progress accumulate. Work stays useful, repairable and adaptable, so it improves with time instead of being replaced.
  3. Make the experience complete. Buying, understanding, using, repairing and getting support are all part of the design.
  4. Let ambition become tangible. Build enough of an idea that people can encounter its value directly.

The tests

Every project starts with two questions, and is judged against them when it ends.

  1. What becomes possible in someone’s life because this exists?
  2. Who can actually share in that possibility?

What EOD will never do

  • Design for failure, or make something to be replaced when it could be repaired.
  • Lock people in by withholding parts, tools or information.
  • Sell the look of the future without the substance.
  • Treat people or places as expendable.
  • Present a concept as a product, or a proposition as a promise.
  • Grow for its own sake.
